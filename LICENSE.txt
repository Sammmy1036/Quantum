Content users share

Prices, station positions, pad sizes, planet alignments, pictures, shared
routes, messages, marketplace listings, org pages, logos and rank icons, and
public profiles are made by Quantum's users, and each user is responsible for
what they share. By uploading a picture or logo, a user confirms they have the
right to share it and agrees that it may be shown to other Quantum users.

The server owner and their moderators can review, reject, replace or remove
anything shared, take down listings, change a username, and suspend or ban an
account that misuses the service. Shared data is provided as is, like the rest
of the Software, and may be inaccurate or out of date.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Marketplace

The marketplace is for in-game trades paid in aUEC only. Real-money trades
(dollars, PayPal, gift cards and the like) break Star Citizen's Terms of
Service and are not allowed; listings that ask for them are taken down.

Trades are between the players involved, who meet and hand over items in
game. Quantum and its server owner are not a party to any trade and give no
guarantee about any listing, player or trade. Escrow agents are volunteer
players, not employees of Quantum; the optional escrow fee is paid in aUEC to
the agent. Ratings and reports help players judge each other, but users trade
at their own risk.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Privacy

How Quantum and the Quantum server handle your data (accounts, usernames,
friends and messages, marketplace, orgs, public profiles, backups and UEX
keys) is described in the privacy policy at https://quantumsc.org/privacy.
Anyone running their own community server receives the same kinds of data
and should handle them with the same care.

Backups are a convenience, provided as is, with no guarantee that they will be
available or complete. Keep your own copy of anything you can't afford to
lose.
